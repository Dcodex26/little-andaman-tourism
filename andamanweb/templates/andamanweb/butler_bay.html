Discover Butler Bay

A Peaceful Coastal Escape

Butler Bay Beach is one of the places that visitors can explore while discovering the natural beauty of Little Andaman. The beach offers a calm coastal atmosphere where the beauty of the sea and surrounding landscape can be enjoyed at a relaxed pace.

Away from the busy feeling of large tourist destinations, Butler Bay provides an opportunity to slow down and enjoy the natural surroundings. The combination of the coastline, open spaces and tropical environment makes it a memorable stop during a Little Andaman trip.

Whether you want to spend some quiet time near the beach, take photographs, enjoy the scenery or simply experience the peaceful side of the island, Butler Bay can be included in your sightseeing plan.

Getting There

How to Reach Butler Bay

🚗

Local Travel

Visitors staying in Little Andaman can arrange local transportation to explore Butler Bay and nearby attractions. Travel time and road conditions can vary, so it is a good idea to confirm the current local route and conditions before starting your journey.

If you are travelling with a local guide or accommodation provider, they can also help you plan the most convenient way to include Butler Bay in your sightseeing schedule.

Travel Tips

Plan Your Visit

A little preparation can make your beach visit more comfortable and enjoyable.

  • Carry sufficient drinking water.
  • Use sun protection during the day.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and footwear.
  • Check local weather and travel conditions.
  • Keep the beach and surrounding area clean.
  • Respect the natural environment.
