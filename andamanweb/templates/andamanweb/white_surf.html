Discover White Surf

A Refreshing Escape Into Nature

White Surf Waterfall is one of the natural attractions that visitors can explore while discovering Little Andaman. Surrounded by greenery, the waterfall provides a refreshing change from the island's coastal destinations.

The sound and movement of flowing water combined with the surrounding natural landscape create a peaceful environment for visitors who enjoy spending time close to nature.

A visit to White Surf Waterfall also adds variety to a Little Andaman sightseeing plan, allowing travellers to experience more than just the island's beaches and coastline.

Getting There

How to Reach White Surf Waterfall

🚗

Local Travel

Visitors staying in Little Andaman can arrange local transportation to explore White Surf Waterfall along with the other sightseeing destinations on the island.

Local travel conditions and access can vary, especially depending on weather. Visitors should confirm current conditions and the local route before beginning their journey.

Accommodation providers or local guides can help visitors plan transportation and include White Surf Waterfall in their sightseeing schedule.

Travel Tips

Plan Your Visit

A little preparation can make your visit to White Surf Waterfall more comfortable and safe.

  • Carry sufficient drinking water.
  • Wear comfortable footwear.
  • Be careful on wet or slippery surfaces.
  • Check local weather conditions before travelling.
  • Avoid disturbing the surrounding natural area.
  • Keep the waterfall and surroundings clean.
